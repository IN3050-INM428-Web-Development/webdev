Our story

A club with a thousand-year story

Al Sah-him Archery Club traces its roots back to 1013 CE, when it began as a gathering of elite marksmen dedicated to mastering the traditional bow.

Under the legendary Master Bowman Talia, the club established rigorous training disciplines and tactical traditions that still influence our practices today.

Over the centuries we have adapted from a medieval combat guild into a modern sporting institution, preserving that ancient focus alongside contemporary techniques. Today the club is based in central London, with weather-sheltered indoor ranges and outdoor shooting spaces.
