Range rules

Safety on the range

Archery is a safe sport when everyone follows the range officer. These rules apply to members, guests and visiting clubs.

  1. Wait for the signal. Only shoot when the range officer gives the command.
  2. Stay behind the line. Never cross the shooting line while anyone is shooting.
  3. Collect arrows together. Only walk forward when the range is declared clear.
  4. Check your kit. Inspect bows, strings and arrows before you shoot; report damage to the range officer.
  5. Nock on the line. Only nock an arrow when you are on the shooting line and cleared to shoot.
  6. Walk, do not run. Watch for arrows in the ground when collecting.
