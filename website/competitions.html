Visiting clubs

Come and compete with us

Al Sah-him Archery Club welcomes archers from other clubs to our central London range. Here is what is coming up and how to enter.

Our fixtures mix indoor and outdoor target archery, from friendly inter-club matches to regional competitions. If you are planning a visit, check the event details below and get in touch before the closing date.

Taking part

How to enter

Every visiting archer needs a current Archery GB card. Entry for away fixtures is managed by the host club; for competitions at Al Sah-him, contact us directly.

  1. Check the details. Make sure the date, round and closing date work for you, and check your Archery GB card is current.
  2. Send your entry. For events at Al Sah-him, email archery@example.org; for away fixtures, use the host club's entry form. Include your name, club, bow style and Archery GB number. [Placeholder: online entry form].
  3. Pay the entry fee. [Placeholder: payment method and deadline].
  4. Wait for confirmation. We will confirm your place and send joining instructions, including arrival time and range rules.

If you need an accessibility adjustment or have a question about the round, tell us when you enter and we will do our best to help.
