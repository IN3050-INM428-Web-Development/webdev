Join the club

Join Al Sah-him Archery Club

Whether you have never held a bow or you are returning to the sport, our beginner courses, junior academies and coaching will help you find your aim.

We are a welcoming and inclusive club in central London. Equipment is provided, the range is weather-sheltered, and there is a clear path from your first session to shooting regularly.

Why archery?

A sport that meets you where you are

Archery is gentle on the joints and demanding on focus. It rewards patience, rewards practice, and gives you a clear way to see your progress.

  • From beginner to competitor

    Junior academies for ages 8–16, adult beginner inductions and advanced masterclasses for tournament archers.

  • Central London facilities

    Weather-sheltered indoor ranges and outdoor shooting spaces, with 18 m indoor lanes and flexible outdoor distances.

  • Coaching that builds confidence

    Structured induction courses and advanced coaching focus on safe technique and steady progress.

  • A club, not just a range

    Seasonal leagues, community social nights and regional competitions give you plenty of reasons to keep shooting.
